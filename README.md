# Town Escape

**Town Escape** is a cooperative multiplayer survival game about getting out of a town that has turned against you.

You and your crew wake up under lockdown. The streets are crawling with AI-controlled locals, patrols, and trackers. Search houses for supplies, make noise only when you can afford to, and reach an extraction point before the town closes in.

## The hook

The town reacts to what the players do. A smashed window, a running generator, or a missing supply cache can change patrol routes and draw hunters toward the crew. Every escape should feel like a plan coming apart in a different way.

## Core loop

1. **Scavenge** a neighborhood for tools, medicine, and clues.
2. **Coordinate** with your team to open routes, distract pursuers, and share scarce supplies.
3. **Adapt** as the town responds to noise, sightings, and time.
4. **Escape** together through one of several risky exits.

## First playable milestone

- Small town map with a few connected streets and interiors
- 2–4 player co-op
- Basic movement, interaction, inventory, and revive
- AI patrols that investigate sounds and chase spotted players
- One objective chain and one extraction point
- A complete win or loss round

## Design principles

- **Teamwork creates options:** players can split up, but helping each other is safer.
- **The town learns locally:** AI reacts to recent events, so players can misdirect it.
- **Tension comes from choices:** loud, fast routes trade time for danger.
- **Escaping is the goal:** combat is a last resort, not the main loop.

## Status

Early concept. The first milestone is intentionally small; engine, networking model, and art direction are still to be chosen.
